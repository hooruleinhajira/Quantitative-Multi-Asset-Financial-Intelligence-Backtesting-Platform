Understanding Volatility vs Downside Risk

Symmetric Volatility Penalizes both upside jumps and downside drops equally
Downside Deviation Focuses purely on harmful downside portfolio fluctuations
Sortino vs Sharpe Sortino > Sharpe indicates volatility is skewed to the upside

Value at Risk (VaR 95%) Interpretation

Historical VaR On 95% of days, loss will not exceed this percentage
Parametric VaR Theoretical loss expected assuming normal distribution
Fat Tail Risk Cryptocurrencies & tech equities typically have higher tail risk
